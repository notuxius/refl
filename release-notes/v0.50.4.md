- Use the arrow keys to move through the sidebar, toolbar, and Options. In the app list, Up and Down select rows and Left and Right scroll when columns extend past the window. Left and Right also switch editor tabs and language-picker columns.
- Tab and Shift+Tab now move between controls in both directions, including out of popup menus and multiline fields. Press **Ctrl+I** to insert a literal tab in a multiline field. You can also tab into read-only item details, release notes, and the list of files in a delete dialog.
- Keyboard focus is easier to see on buttons, Options choices, and the selected row in the app list. The sidebar toggle also keeps its spacing when switching between compact and expanded layouts.
